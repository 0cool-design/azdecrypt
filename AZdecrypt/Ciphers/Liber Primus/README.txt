Liber Primus ciphers for AZdecrypt.

Each file is one page (group), given as space-separated Gematria Primus symbol
numbers 0-28 (one number per rune). This is the format AZdecrypt reads correctly;
the raw UTF-8 rune glyphs load as garbled single-byte characters (an encoding
issue, not a font issue), so numbers are used instead.

Rune order (index -> rune/Latin): 0 F, 1 U, 2 TH, 3 O, 4 R, 5 C, 6 G, 7 W, 8 H,
9 N, 10 I, 11 J, 12 EO, 13 P, 14 X, 15 S, 16 T, 17 B, 18 E, 19 M, 20 L, 21 NG,
22 OE, 23 D, 24 A, 25 AE, 26 Y, 27 IA, 28 EA.

The raw rune transcriptions live in Source/cicada/data/pages/ for use with the
cicada toolkit.
